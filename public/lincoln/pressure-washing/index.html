Will pressure washing damage my siding, deck or roof?

No. We use low-pressure soft-wash methods on siding, roofs and other delicate surfaces, so they get clean without being blasted.

How long does it take?

Most house washing and driveway jobs are done in a few hours. We'll give you a timeline up front.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. For most exterior jobs we just need access to the areas we're cleaning and a water source.

How often should a house be washed?

Most homes do well with a full exterior cleaning every 1 to 2 years, depending on shade, trees and weather. We can suggest a schedule for yours.

Do you come to Lincoln?

Yes. We're based in Beatrice and proudly serve Lincoln. Not sure you're in our area? Call and we'll tell you.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're fully licensed, bonded and insured, so you're protected on every job.

Cash, check and major credit cards. If you need a payment arrangement on a bigger job, we're happy to talk through options.
